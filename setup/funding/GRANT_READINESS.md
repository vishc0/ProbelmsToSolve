## MVP evidence package

Prepare once and adapt per program:

- functioning public website and domain email;
- concise problem, beneficiary, and business/public-value statement;
- live or recorded vertical-slice demonstration;
- architecture and security diagram;
- reproducible benchmark and current resource baseline;
- requested CPU/GPU/storage quantities with workload math;
- 6–12 month milestones and measurable outcomes;
- open-source, publication, or community impact plan where applicable;
- budget, credit burn-down, shutdown controls, and post-credit sustainability;
- incorporation, funding, or academic-affiliation evidence when required.

## Application order

1. Finish the zero-spend local vertical slice.
2. Publish the MVP site and one reference artifact.
3. Apply to programs whose eligibility already fits; do not incorporate or spend
   merely to chase credits without a separate owner decision.
4. Use small awards to produce measured usage and outcomes.
5. Apply for larger GPU allocations with evidence rather than estimates alone.
