## Executive Overview

AI Futures Project presents **Plan A** as a concrete, normative scenario to avert an uncoordinated race to Artificial Superintelligence (ASI). Contrasting with unconstrained racing (Plan D) or unilateral kinetic/cyber sabotage (Plan B), Plan A proposes an international coalition (spearheaded by the US and China) to:
1. Implement a **verified slowdown** of frontier AI capabilities development until 2040.
2. Establish **Total Research Transparency**, requiring frontier algorithmic techniques, models, and safety cases to be made open or auditable.
3. Restrict capability advancement to **Top-Expert-Dominating AI (TED-AI)** between 2030 and 2035, followed by a 5-year pause at human-expert levels before contemplating controlled handoff.
4. Institute **Mutually Assured Compute Destruction (MACD)** using remote/maritime datacenter deployments and cryptographic self-destruct triggers to deter defection.
