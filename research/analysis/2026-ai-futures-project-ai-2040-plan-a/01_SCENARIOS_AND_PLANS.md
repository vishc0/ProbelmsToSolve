# Plan A & Alternative Strategic Scenarios

This document compares the primary policy scenarios evaluated by the AI Futures Project in *AI 2040*, contrasting the cooperative slowdown model (Plan A) against alternative defensive, competitive, and emergency pathways.

---

## 1. Scenario Taxonomy

The AI Futures Project models six distinct pathways for navigating the transition between Automated Coder (AC) and Superintelligence (ASI):

| Plan               | Name / Archetype                           | Strategic Mechanism                                                                                                                  | Projected Slowdown                        |
| :----------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| **Plan A**         | **Verified Slowdown & Total Transparency** | Bilateral US-China treaty, chip registry, hardware cap-and-trade, packet verification, and full research transparency.               | **~6 Years** (AC to ASI deferred to 2040) |
| **Plan B-Kinetic** | **Kinetic Sabotage & Lead Burning**        | US unilaterally strikes adversary datacenter/energy infrastructure using kinetic/drone strikes to maintain lead and buy safety time. | **~3 Years**                              |
| **Plan B-Cyber**   | **Non-Kinetic Cyber Sabotage**             | US executes offensive cyber/supply-chain operations against adversary clusters without kinetic escalation; lead spent on safety.     | **~2 Years**                              |
| **Plan C+**        | **Domestic Regulation (No Sabotage)**      | US enacts heavy domestic frontier licensing, strict export controls, and talent acquisition; no aggressive sabotage against China.   | **~1.5 Years**                            |
| **Plan C**         | **Leading Project Lead-Burning**           | Private frontier labs voluntarily coordinate to delay deployment and burn lead on safety without comprehensive state regulation.     | **~1.13 Years**                           |
| **Plan D**         | **Unconstrained Race to ASI**              | Frontier labs race at maximum velocity through the intelligence explosion; minimal (<1%) safety tax.                                 | **~1.02 Years** (~12 months)              |
| **Plan S**         | **Emergency Indefinite Halt**              | Global emergency shutdown of all frontier compute clusters pending formal alignment or whole-brain emulation breakthroughs.          | **Indefinite**                            |

## 4. The Plan A Timeline (2026–2040)

- **2026–2028 (Phase 1 — Preparation & Awakening):**
  - AI agents automate high-volume coding and white-collar tasks. Datacenters reach $100B+ valuations.
  - Congressional hearings expose lack of control mechanisms. AI Transparency Act mandates reporting of internal vs. external capability gaps.
  - Verification R&D initiates (inference-only retrofits, packet sampling).
- **2029 (Phase 2 — The Multilateral Accord):**
  - US and China sign bilateral AI Accord.
  - Mutual chip declaration audits world compute stock (~289M H100e total: US 77%, China 9%, Rest of World 14%). Dark compute bounded to <1%.
  - Frontier capability training pauses while verification infrastructure is deployed.
- **2030–2034 (Phase 3 — Verified Scaling Within Human Range):**
  - R&D resumes under strict safety cases and Total Research Transparency.
  - Scaling is restricted to human-expert capability levels (up to TED-AI).
  - Software progress is deliberately suppressed to prevent leakage to covert projects (~0.2 OOM software per OOM hardware).
- **2035 (Phase 4 — The Mid-Point Pause & Ocean Datacenters):**
  - Capabilities reach Top-Expert-Dominating AI (TED-AI). All further frontier scaling is paused.
  - Datacenters transition to international waters (floating/ocean platforms) to ensure Mutually Assured Compute Destruction (MACD).
- **2040 (Phase 5 — Handoff or Superintelligence):**
  - With alignment verification and robust untrusted monitoring proven at 99.5%+ confidence, the consortium unpauses to scale toward safe superintelligence.
