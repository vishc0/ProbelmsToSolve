# Problem Cluster: Denial by Default

> Institutions benefit when people abandon valid disputes after an initial denial.

## Shared root cause

Organizations can transfer costs to individuals by rejecting claims or requests first, because pursuing a correction demands time, knowledge, and persistence while abandonment is profitable or administratively convenient.

## Examples

- Insurance claim denials and rarely pursued appeals.
- Credit disputes and withheld deposit returns.
