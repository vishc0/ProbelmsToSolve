# Problem Cluster: Fine Print and Information Asymmetry

> Organizations hold the data, specialist knowledge, and pricing visibility needed to understand consequential terms.

## Shared root cause

One party controls the contract language, records, expert interpretation, or price construction, leaving ordinary people unable to assess the full consequences at the point of decision.

## Examples

- Leases, loans, and hospital bills.
- Pharmacy-benefit rebates and trade rules.
