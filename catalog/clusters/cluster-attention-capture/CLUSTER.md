# Problem Cluster: Attention Capture

> Engagement systems monetize impulses and sustained attention rather than supporting user-chosen goals.

## Shared root cause

Business models reward systems for maximizing engagement, so product optimization favors repeated impulses and dependency even when those outcomes conflict with a person's longer-term intentions.

## Examples

- Betting applications and infinite feeds.
- Companion bots optimized for engagement.
