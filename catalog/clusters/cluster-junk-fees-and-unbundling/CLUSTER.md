# Problem Cluster: Junk Fees and Unbundling

> Sellers advertise a low headline price and reveal mandatory or hard-to-avoid fees late in the transaction.

## Shared root cause

Separating unavoidable charges from the advertised price makes offers appear cheaper and exploits the cost of restarting a decision after a person has invested time or reached the point of no return.

## Examples

- Rental add-ons and hospital facility fees.
- Bank fees.
