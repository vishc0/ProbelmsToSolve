# Problem Cluster: Paperwork Churn

> Procedural complexity removes eligible people from benefits and services through avoidable administrative failure.

## Shared root cause

Repeated forms, deadlines, proof requirements, and fragmented administration shift the burden of continuity onto individuals, allowing procedural mistakes to override substantive eligibility.

## Examples

- Medicaid and SNAP renewals.
- Disability-benefit appeals.
