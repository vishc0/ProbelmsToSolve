# Problem Cluster: Weak Global Cooperation

> Institutional vetoes, weighted power, and stalled dispute mechanisms prevent timely collective action.

## Shared root cause

International institutions embed unequal blocking power and depend on member consent without sufficiently credible enforcement, allowing shared problems and formal commitments to remain unresolved.

## Examples

- UN Security Council vetoes.
- Stalled WTO appeals and contested IMF quota representation.
