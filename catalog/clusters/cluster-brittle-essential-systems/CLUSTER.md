# Problem Cluster: Brittle Essential Systems

> Capital-intensive essential systems concentrate control and retain single points of failure.

## Shared root cause

High capital requirements, long replacement cycles, and concentrated supply or operational control leave essential systems dependent on components and institutions that are difficult to substitute quickly.

## Examples

- Electricity grids, nuclear generation, and storage.
- Factory equipment and food-system inputs.
