# Problem Cluster: Concentrated Gatekeepers

> A small number of providers control essential access, leaving people with few practical alternatives.

## Shared root cause

Market concentration, exclusive rights, and high switching or entry costs let a few actors set terms for essential goods and services without effective competitive discipline.

## Examples

- Grocery access and rent-pricing systems.
- Drug patents and freight carriers.
