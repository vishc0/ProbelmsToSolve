# Problem Cluster: Easy In, Hard Out

> Enrollment is streamlined while cancellation, switching, and repayment exits carry deliberate friction.

## Shared root cause

Providers gain recurring revenue or leverage when joining is effortless but leaving requires extra steps, precise timing, repeated contact, or costs that were not salient at entry.

## Examples

- Subscription cancellation and free-trial rollovers.
- Loan rollovers.
