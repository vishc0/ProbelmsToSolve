# Problem: Unverifiable Compute Execution

External reviewers cannot cheaply confirm that governed compute followed
declared workloads and controls.

## Affected parties

Operators, auditors, regulators, and communities affected by high-risk compute
need evidence without disclosing complete workloads.

## Root-cause hypothesis

Compute execution is opaque, while full recomputation is too expensive. This
record connects the pain point to the opaque-compute-activity cluster.

## Success condition

An independent reviewer can obtain proportionate, testable evidence of
compliance with bounded cost and disclosed uncertainty.
