# Opportunity: Open Governance Observatory

> A neutral, reproducible observatory for institutional vetoes, commitments, and cooperation outcomes.

## Problem

Public decisions and commitments across international institutions are difficult to compare because records, definitions, and outcomes are fragmented.

## People

Researchers, educators, journalists, civil-society groups, and members of the public seeking inspectable institutional history.

## Root cause

Veto powers, weighted representation, and stalled dispute mechanisms operate across separate sources without a shared, reproducible view.

## Tool response

Build an open data pipeline and static site that records veto events and public commitments with source links, methods, definitions, and limitations.

## Governance response

Publish neutral analysis only, separate facts from interpretation, review dataset licences, and use transparent correction and methodology policies.

## Prior art

UNCTAD maritime reviews, IMF financial statistics, WTO dispute-settlement databases, SIPRI military-expenditure data, UNDP Human Development Reports, and the Social Progress Index cover parts of the problem separately. The brief identifies the gap as the absence of one open, neutral telemetry platform connecting structural-power measures with wellbeing outcomes through reproducible methods.

## Guardrails

Avoid advocacy scoring, predictions about conflict, inferred intent, moralizing, and national favoritism. Minimize personal data and never offer legal or medical advice. Audit every dataset's licence: SIPRI fair use requires a non-commercial purpose and less than 10% reproduction; UNDP indicators require CC BY 3.0 IGO attribution; Social Progress Index terms prohibit external derivative products or redistribution without written permission.

## Success measure

A reader can reproduce a published view from licensed source data and trace each displayed event or commitment to its source.
