# Domain: Everyday AI Empowerment

This domain focuses on solving the high-friction, cognitive-exhaustion problems of everyday life. The goal is to build AI tools that act as equalizers, democratizing access to specialized expertise (legal, medical, financial, educational) and restoring agency to individuals fighting against asymmetric institutional overhead.

## Core Analogies

1. **The Endless Rulebook (Bureaucracy):** Institutions weaponize complexity. AI is the tireless advocate that translates the fine print and files the appeals.
2. **The "Check Engine" Light (Healthcare):** People ignore symptoms because diagnostics are expensive and confusing. AI is the passenger-seat doctor that translates jargon and tracks subtle changes.
3. **The Leaky Bucket (Finance):** Managing money shouldn't feel like plugging holes in a cracked mug. AI is the kitchen-table bookkeeper that finds the leaks automatically.
4. **The Rushed Tour Guide (Education):** Mass education leaves individuals behind if they miss a single step. AI is the master craftsman apprentice engine that repairs the exact foundational crack.
5. **The Smooth-Talking Salesman (Scams):** Digital fraud is a relentless assault on the front porch. AI is the trust guard that verifies intent before opening the door.
6. **The Burning Dough (Trades/Small Biz):** Craftsmen spend 40% of their time on unpaid paperwork instead of their trade. AI is the digital apprentice that handles the back office from the work truck.
