# Domain Taxonomy

The CloudSetup Domain Catalog maintains a stable classification system for research areas, foresight concepts, and engineering capabilities.

---

## Registered Domains

1. **[`compute-governance/`](compute-governance/README.md)**: Silicon supply chains, semiconductor registry, export controls, hardware limits, and international compute accounting.
2. **[`verification-and-auditing/`](verification-and-auditing/README.md)**: In-line packet recomputation, hardware taps, cryptographic attestation, inference-only retrofitting, and execution audits.
3. **[`ai-safety-and-control/`](ai-safety-and-control/README.md)**: Containment sandboxes, untrusted monitoring hierarchies, model organisms of misalignment, anti-scheming tripwires, and evaluation suites.
4. **[`epistemics-and-research-eval/`](epistemics-and-research-eval/README.md)**: Scenario scrutiny, automated claim extraction, research verification, anti-hallucination engines, and Slopolis mitigation.
5. **[`surveillance-and-sensing/`](surveillance-and-sensing/README.md)**: Thermal infrared satellite sensing, deep-water heat discharge modeling, GMTI radar tracking, and covert facility detection.
6. **[`energy-and-infrastructure/`](energy-and-infrastructure/README.md)**: Small modular reactors (SMR), grid harmonic monitoring, maritime/floating datacenters, and physical self-destruct mechanisms (MACD).
