# Solution Pattern: Personal Safety Guard

> Detect manipulation and fraud locally while preserving user agency and privacy.

## Applicable context

Use for messages, offers, or engagement patterns where a person benefits from a pause, an explanation of warning signs, and independent verification steps.

## Pattern

Analyze content locally, identify concrete signals, explain uncertainty, suggest reversible checks, and leave the final decision with the user.

## Tradeoffs and failure modes

False alarms can erode trust, missed detections can create misplaced confidence, and behavior guidance can drift into inappropriate health claims.

## Safety and cost boundaries

Never guarantee safety, diagnose a condition, or manipulate the user; protect local data and point to qualified help when stakes are high.
