# Solution Pattern: Open Interoperability

> Use shared schemas and protocols so independent actors can coordinate without a single platform owner.

## Applicable context

Use where fragmented logistics or mobility actors need selective data exchange but should retain control of their systems and commercial relationships.

## Pattern

Define minimal shared identifiers and events, publish conformance tests, support incremental adoption, and keep implementations substitutable.

## Tradeoffs and failure modes

Standards can entrench incumbents, leak sensitive business data, or fail when incentives for accurate participation are weak.

## Safety and cost boundaries

Minimize shared data, avoid centralized lock-in, document governance, and provide a zero-spend reference implementation.
